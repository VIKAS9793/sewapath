Accessibility at SewaPath

The public portal is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets, and laptop or desktop screens, with clear language choices and visible focus states.

Current support

  • Responsive layout for narrow mobile, tablet, and wide desktop screens.
  • Marathi, Hindi, and English entry points.
  • Semantic headings, labelled controls, keyboard focus indicators, and descriptive links.
  • A text-based guide remains available if JavaScript is unavailable.
  • No document upload is required to use the preparation flow.

Known limits

This is an early prototype. Voice interaction is demonstrated rather than recorded, and some language copy may be improved through testing. The official portal is a separate website with its own accessibility experience.
