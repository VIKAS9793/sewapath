Safety and non-competence disclaimer

SewaPath is designed to reduce confusion, not to act as an authority or make decisions for a citizen.

For urgent or high-risk situations: use the appropriate official emergency, police, medical, legal, or government channel. SewaPath is not an emergency service.

We do not decide

  • Eligibility, entitlement, income, residency, or document sufficiency.
  • Whether an application will be accepted, rejected, approved, or delayed.
  • Whether a government officer, service centre, or private person has acted unlawfully.
  • What legal, medical, financial, or safety action a citizen should take.

Use official information for the final decision

Government rules, forms, fees, document requirements, and processing timelines can change. Treat SewaPath’s checklist as a preparation aid only and verify the current official instructions before acting.
