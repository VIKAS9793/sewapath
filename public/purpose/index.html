About this service

Purpose and independence

SewaPath reduces uncertainty before a citizen opens an official public-service portal.

Non-competence disclaimer: SewaPath does not issue certificates, decide eligibility, interpret a person’s legal rights, accept applications, or promise an outcome.

What SewaPath does

  • Explains a public-service journey in simpler language.
  • Offers Marathi, Hindi, and English starting points.
  • Shows preparation categories without asking citizens to upload documents.
  • Links to the official service destination for the actual application.
  • Collects only anonymous, aggregate friction signals when a visitor explicitly allows analytics.

What SewaPath does not do

  • It is not a Government of Maharashtra website or service centre.
  • It is not a substitute for current official instructions.
  • It does not verify documents, calculate income, or determine eligibility.
  • It does not provide emergency, legal, financial, medical, or professional advice.
  • It does not publish accusations or track individual complaints.

Why the official link matters

The official portal remains the source for current forms, documents, fees, timelines, status, and submission rules. SewaPath’s role ends at helping a citizen reach that route with better preparation.
