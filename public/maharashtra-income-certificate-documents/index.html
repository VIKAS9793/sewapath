Maharashtra public-service preparation

Maharashtra income certificate: documents and online route

Use this short guide to understand what to prepare before opening the official Aaple Sarkar service list.

Important: SewaPath is an independent navigation aid. It is not the Government of Maharashtra, does not issue certificates, and cannot decide eligibility.

Common preparation categories

  • Identity proof
  • Address proof showing the relevant Maharashtra address
  • Income proof that matches your family’s situation
  • Photograph or self-declaration if the official form asks for it

Before you submit

  • Use the current official service instructions, not an old checklist.
  • Check that names and addresses are consistent across documents.
  • Keep originals available if verification is requested.
  • Save the official application or acknowledgement number.

Where to apply

The official Maharashtra service route is provided through Aaple Sarkar. SewaPath helps you prepare and then hands you to the official service destination.

Open the official Aaple Sarkar service list

Need assisted help?

If self-service is difficult, use an authorised assisted-service centre or local government help channel. Never share an OTP, password, or original document with an untrusted person.

The checklist above is a preparation aid, not a legal or eligibility determination. Verify current documents, fees, timelines, and submission rules on the official portal.
